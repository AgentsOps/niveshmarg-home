What these results can't tell you.

Survivorship bias
Back-filling today's large caps flatters long-only results. We add names that left the index, but the effect is not zero.
Small samples
A handful of trades per year leaves wide confidence intervals. We report them rather than hide them.
Regimes change
A strategy that held up for a decade can stop working. That is why passing means paper first.
